Delete your Jackpot Arena account and data

App: Jackpot Arena · Developer contact: joshmcdouh@gmail.com

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Jackpot Arena.
  2. In the lobby, tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm twice.

Your account is deleted immediately and the app returns to its first-launch screen.

Option 2: by email

Email joshmcdouh@gmail.com with the subject “Jackpot Arena data deletion”. Because accounts are anonymous, include anything that helps us find yours (roughly when you installed the app, your approximate coin balance, recent bets). We complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account and profile
  • Your coin balance and full coin history, including rewarded-ad credits
  • All slots, blackjack, poker and sportsbook (single and parlay) records

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept on our servers. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's policies and your device's ad settings.
